Command Center 22 Briefing

DEPLOYMENT TIMELINE: THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 24TH, 2026 (APPROX. 17:00 PDT)
HEADQUARTERS PROGRESSION: Command Center Level 22 unlocks modern defensive platforms, tech trees, and facility tiers.
UPGRADE TIME: 8 DAYS
RESOURCE: 80,000 IRIDIUM

Mine Factory Overhaul

  • Deployable Minefields: Replaces individual mine placements. Matches the exact footprint and structure sizing of a heavy platform.
  • Stealth & Auto-Rearm: Remains undetected by enemy forces until triggered or unmasked by specialized sweepers. Auto-rearms post-battle based on mine lifespan. Instant arming remains available via Gold.
  • Tactical Munition Variants: Damage mines now inflict balanced flat damage. New variants introduce status effects and summoning capabilities. New mine variants will drop via HOLDOUT.
  • Global Upgrades & HUD: All planted fields automatically scale to your highest unlocked tier in the Mine Factory. Management and placement are streamlined through a redesigned interface.

Auxiliary Base Assets EXPANDED
Expanded caps for Academy, Tech Center, Air Hangar, Construction Yard, and Heavy Platforms.

Tactical Logistics & Quality of Life

  • Decentralized Research: Research modules moved to their direct structural sources: Command Center, Defense Lab, Missile Silo, and Mine Factory.
  • Enhanced Inventory Display: Refreshed inventory dashboard. Hovering over any stockpiled material displays descriptions and source acquisition methods.
  • Contractor Invulnerability: Contractors are now untargetable by hostiles, adopting the behavior of base trophies.
  • Combat Engine Fixes: Resolved an infinite loop bug in unit firing animations. Contrast and legibility updated for the Building List and Recon Satellite screens.
