SEASON 60 OVERVIEW & INFAMY MECHANICS

PvP Season 60 launches immediately following downtime on Thursday, November 12, 2026 @ 11:00 AM PST and runs for 48 days through Wednesday, December 30, 2026 @ 10:00 AM PST. Compete across Individual Infamy Divisions and Alliance Leaderboards to earn massive Medal caches and Blood Thorium rewards.

Don't miss your chance to secure high-value tactical reinforcements at a special price before they rotate out. Check the in-game offers tab right now to claim yours before time runs out!

💧 Infamy Drip

Earn Infamy in between attacks. A percentage of a player’s Infamy is dripped back to them, only when they are unbubbled and their base is active. The more Infamy a player has, the more that will drip back.
