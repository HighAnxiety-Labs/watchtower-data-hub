MISSION OVERVIEW

Earn the next Arc II Hero,  Midway, the  Rangers Hero in this month’s Skirmish, and use it to dominate in the upcoming monthly Event. 

Use  Nomad, Ironside, Valiant MkII, and Sabre units and their  Heroes, against  Ryu-Kai to play through the Recruit set or the Officer set to earn skirmish XP. Sets can be completed in any order and pay out a bonus XP Cache. The Officer set will test your might but will pay out more XP than the Recruit set. Choose the set that best fits your power level. You can hit the targets multiple times, and wins will count towards future set completions, so you won't be penalized for hitting a target again!
