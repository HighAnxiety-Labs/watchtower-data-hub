3. High-Value Assets & Profiles

Unique Unit: Baron Anansi

Liberated child soldier under Mutoto who deserted the Eastern Horde. A specialist in unmanned combat systems, Anansi formerly petitioned AI Onyx before breaking off due to machine-human dissonance. Currently seeking drone autonomy from rogue AI platforms while assessing tactical alignment with RUBI. Earn 3 components across the operation phases, with the final component unlocking in the November Main Event.

Adversary Profile: ONYX

Former core system of a global preservation AI grid, currently operating autonomously with aggressive expansionist goals. Remnants of mutual integration with sister module RUBI remain fractured.
