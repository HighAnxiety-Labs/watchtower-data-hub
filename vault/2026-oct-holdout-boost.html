Holdout Base Details Combat Regulations
  • Scalable Targets: Three main Holdout bases are discovered on the world map, scaling progressively in tactical difficulty.
  • Continuous Combat: No 3-wave partitions. Enemy assaults are continuous gauntlets with short intermittent gaps in incoming waves.
  • Survive to the End: Battles conclude solely when enemy forces are exhausted. Watch in-game stage alerts to monitor mission completion.
  • All-or-Nothing Victory: Partial progression credit and UI pips have been removed. Survival through the entire assault is required to collect victory payouts.
  • Elite Bonus Base: Conquering all 3 primary targets unlocks the high-tier Gauntlet Bonus Base.

Progression & Resources Tokens & Tactical Assets
  • Holdout Experience: Earn event XP through destroyed base crates and first-time full clears. Resetting allows you to re-earn first-time clear bonuses.
  • Free Track Resets: Successfully defeating Base 3 grants unlimited free track resets to refresh targets and XP payouts.
  • Restart Tokens: All commanders deploy with 3 free Restart Tokens at event start. Additional tokens replenish progressively every 15 minutes.

Field Deployment Rules Event Mechanic
  • ▶ HOW TO CLAIM To unlock a crate, simply command one of your ground units to stand directly over it.
