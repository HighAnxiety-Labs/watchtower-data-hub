WARPATH (OCT 2026)

Starts: FRI OCT 16, 2026 @ 10:00 AM PDT Ends: TUE OCT 20, 2026 @ 10:00 AM PDT
WARPATH OVERVIEW Combat Regulations
  • Engage in Player-vs-Player (PvP) combat to earn Honor. Winning Honor improves your position on the Leaderboard. Reach higher Leaderboard Divisions to unlock Medals and Blood Thorium.
  • Honor Mechanics: Honor earned is based on your opponent's level and is no longer influenced by the attacker's level.
  • Exchange Rules: The difference between the attacker's and defender's Honor is reflected in the exchange. Winning an attack against a player with more Honor than you will result in more Honor won.
  • Repair Times: All Repairs during Warpath are half-time!
  • Protection Rules: Attack individual players twice while Warpath is active. Standard Damage Protection times are set to 4 hours for the duration of Warpath.
DAILY REWARD TRACK PvP Chests
  • In addition to the final prize payout, you can earn Medals and Blood Thorium daily! Every PvP Attack will earn you points toward the track. Each tier unlocks a chest.

    When completed, you have a 12 hour cooldown, or you can use gold to speed it up. Resetting the track costs 25 Gold.
  • Bronze Chest: 20,000 Medals
  • Silver Chest: 40,000 Medals
  • Gold Chest: 80,000 Medals + 2 Blood Thorium (Payout Doubled!)
