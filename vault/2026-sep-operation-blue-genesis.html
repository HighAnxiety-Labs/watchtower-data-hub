SITUATION REPORT

Rubi has been threatened for years, but Onyx just pulled a card that no one expected. A long hidden piece of Onyx inside Rubi was activated, and we had to shut her down. Now with Rubi out of commission, the Allied Factions need to come together and find a way to move forward. Can they find a way to cleanse Rubi of her dark side? Or has she been lost to Onyx?

In Operation:  Blue Genesis, Commanders will take to the battlefield in the monthly event that includes Basic Training Targets, The Incursion Track, the Tactical Track, and the Proving Grounds. Unlock the story in the Incursion and Tactical tracks, and earn HUGE amounts of XP. Use the event XP to purchase prizes in the Event Store. 

Mission Tracks

Basic Training

The Basic Training Bases are designed to be attacked with Units available in the Supply Store or better. 

Basic Training Base Rewards : Holdout style target that awards 200 XP + 4 random Crates that spawn in base that will award 200 additional XP per Crate. For the first 4 times completing this Target, you will receive a Voidhammer, Broadside , and Build Logistics for both. After the 4th completion, you will only receive XP.

Incursion Base Set

This set of bases is designed for a  Platoon of the  newest Unit being offered in the current Main Event  (Achilles), and only  Corpus Units can be deployed. This set will also contain the story for the Main Event.

Base I 1,000 XP
Base II (unlocked after completing base I) 1,500 XP
Base III (unlocked after completing base II) 2,000 XP
Set Completion Bonus Rewards 3,500 XP
Tactical Strike Set

The Tactical Strike Set is unlocked after completing all 3 Incursion Bases, and are more difficult. This Set is designed for Platoons of the Previous Units being offered in the current Main Event ( Voidhammer ) and IS NOT faction locked. Each base will also contain a single High Value Target that will award an additional 4,000 XP . Be careful though, this target knows you’re gunning for it and will attempt to escape the battle if you can’t kill them in time. This set will also contain the story for the Main Event.

Base I (unlocked after completing Incursion Set) 2,000 XP
Base II (unlocked after completing base I) 2,000 XP
Set Completion Bonus Rewards 4,000 XP

Proving Grounds & Chests 

This Target is designed for the 2 most recent  Unique Units. You can bring in a platoon of 2  Unique Units into these bases. As you complete these Targets, you’ll fill up a Progress Bar that allows you to reach a  Bronze, Silver, or Gold Prize Chest. You compete to the level you want, then once you’ve reached the level of Chest you want, click the LOCK IN button on the Event Screen to receive the Chest you’ve earned and to reset your progress. If you are full on the item that you roll on for your award, you will be granted  Thorium as a fallback prize.

BRONZE CHEST
x1.2 XP Boost (2H) and a Random Roll of the following: Repair Logistic, 6K Iridium, or 10K Adv Mats.
SILVER CHEST (Will replace BRONZE BOOST)
x1.5 XP Boost (2H) and a Random Roll of the following: Repair Logistics, 12K Iridium, or 20K Adv Mats.
GOLD CHEST (Will replace SILVER BOOST)
x2.0 XP Boost (2H) and a Random Roll of the following: Premium Repair Logistics, 18K Iridium, or 30K Adv Mats.
