Born into the lawless frostlands, Mama Burnes survived by transforming a ragtag group of abandoned orphans into the territory’s most feared, tight-knit militia. When the Rangers absorbed her syndicate into their military machine, she didn't step down — she simply brought her "family" along, bringing order to the chaotic front lines with an iron fist and a warm hearth. Today, she still treats the warzone like her personal neighborhood, fiercely protecting those who swear fealty to her while quietly adding the dog tags of the fallen to her armor as a reminder that loyalty goes both ways.
