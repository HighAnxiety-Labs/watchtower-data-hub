🌎 SECTION 1: GLOBAL NAVIGATION

HUD VISIBILITY & CAMERA OPTICS
H

Hide / Show HUD

Toggles the Heads-Up Display overlay on or off for unobstructed battlefield visibility and clean recon screenshots.

MOUSE
WHEEL

Zoom In / Out

Adjusts tactical camera elevation across Base and Combat views. (Works everywhere except the World Map.)

⚔️ SECTION 2: IN-COMBAT COMMANDS

DEPLOY THESE SPLIT-SECOND TACTICS WHEN MANAGING BASE OR PLATOON BATTLES
A

Attack Move

Orders selected units to advance toward the destination and automatically engage any hostile threats along the way.

S

Stop / Stall

Immediately halts land units in their tracks or stalls fixed-wing aircraft in place.

D

Hold Position

Forces units to anchor down at their current coordinates and defend their spot without chasing targets.

F

Fire at Will

Directs units to freely acquire and engage any hostile target within weapon range.

M

Move

Issues a standard movement command to a targeted location without stopping to engage along the route.

X

Spread Units

Immediately disperses clustered forces outward to mitigate incoming splash or Area-of-Effect (AoE) damage.

B

Show Enemy HP

Instantly displays red health bars over all opposing forces and structures on the battlefield.

R

Rubi On / Off

Toggles the tactical Rubi targeting and range overlay helper during combat operations.

👥 SQUAD GROUPING SHORTCUTS

RAPID MULTI-SQUAD CONTROL GROUPS
SHIFT + [1–0]

Assign Control Group

Binds the currently selected squad or unit selection to a target number key (1 through 0) for instant recall.

[1 – 0]

Select Control Group

Immediately recalls and selects the squad bound to that number key so you can issue split-second movement or fire orders.

🗺️ SECTION 3: WORLD MAP OPERATIONS

SECTOR NAVIGATION, COORDINATE SHARING & PLATOON ENGAGEMENTS
🧭 NAV & COORDINATE SHARING
Arrow Keys / Drag Move camera view around the World Map
M Opens "Call Platoon" menu to deploy units
SHIFT + ENTER (Hover Hex) Auto-posts in chat: "Target Location: (###,###)"
🎯 WORLD MAP PLATOON ATTACKS
Ctrl + Click Hex Deploy platoon directly for attack
Alt + Click Platoon Select platoon in 'Move' mode
Ctrl + Click Target Set selected platoon's attack target
Ctrl + Click Platoon Select platoon in 'Attack Move' mode
⚠️
NO SCOUTING REQUIREMENT

If the target hex contains an enemy base or platoon, the battle starts and auto-deploys immediately when your platoon arrives on location.
