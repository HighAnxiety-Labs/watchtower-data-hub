PERMANENT COMBAT REWORK RULES

Fire and Energy damage types have been removed. All weapons previously utilizing them have been migrated to the three core types and granted replacement perks:

  • ENERGY All former energy weapons now grant 25% Flat Damage Reduction Piercing.
  • FIRE All former fire weapons now deal a 2x Damage Boost against Zombies and Buildings.
  • REMOVED Weapon Tech has been removed entirely — thorium armor, solar damage, and related tech trees no longer require tracking.
