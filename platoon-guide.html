⚙️ CAPACITY & OPERATIONAL LIMITATIONS

  • Combat Engagement: Units produced in the base must be assigned to platoons to engage in battle. Platoon capacity is governed by the level of your Storage Building, Helipad, or Airfield.
  • Base Assaults (Mix & Match): When attacking an enemy base, commanders have access to all platoons in direct contact with that target on the map and can mix and match squads across platoons up to the 3,400 capacity cap.
  • Destruction & Recovery: Platoons destroyed in combat automatically return to the home base for repair and refit.
  • ⚠️ Map Rules (Anti-Jailing): Circling an enemy player base completely (Jailing) is restricted — at least 1 adjacent hex must remain open. Friendly bases may still be completely encircled for defensive perimeters.
